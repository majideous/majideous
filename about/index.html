About Me

My Background

Hi! I'm Majid, also known on internet as Majideous. I'm an Indonesian currently living in my home country, Indonesia.

I'm passionate about science and technology. The massive growth of science and technology made me want to broaden my knowledge and skills. I'm very much driven by curiosity. I've always been interested in the intersection of biology and computer science.

After I graduated from high school, I spent my time studying biomedical engineering, specialized in medical laboratory technologies for three years, it gave me opportunity to learn new things about wet lab and medical biology, both theoretically and practically. I also have an interest in dry lab.

Then, I spent my time studying computer science for four years while working on a hospital as medical worker. After that, I had an opportunity to gain experience working as an IT support officer. Currently, I'm seeking opportunities that will allow me to make some positive impacts for company and industry through data-driven and software-based solutions. Specific fields of interest include web development, management information system, user interface and experience, and data analytics.

In my spare time, I read articles, blog posts, and forum threads. I also like trying out software, services, and technologies in my free time while listening to my music playlist. When I have plenty of time, I like to read books, either fiction or non-fiction. I also like movies, tv series, video games, anime, manga. Beyond my hobbies in media consumption, I like to code, write essays, photograph, and taking care a diverse collection of fish in my aquarium.

I'm following my own interests, going deeper down the rabbit hole, and surfacing something for me and others.
